Victory Primary School
Ngcobo, Eastern Cape

"At Victory Primary, every child is known by name and loved by God — and we never forget that."

Welcome to Victory Primary School. Since our founding in 2017, we have been committed to offering the children of Ngcobo an education that is rooted in Christian values and delivered with passion and dedication.

We believe that each learner is uniquely gifted by God, and our role as educators is to help them discover and develop those gifts. Our staff pour their hearts into every classroom, every day.

Whether your child is joining us in Grade R for the first time, or completing their primary school journey in Grade 7, they will find at Victory Primary a warm, safe, and faith-filled community that truly cares about their growth — academically, spiritually, and personally.

We are so proud of what we have built together with our families and the Ngcobo community, and we look forward to welcoming many more children into the Victory family.

God bless you and your family.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality, Christ-centred education that equips every learner in Ngcobo with the academic knowledge, moral character, and spiritual foundation they need to succeed in life and serve their community.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of educational excellence and Christian values in Ngcobo and the greater Eastern Cape — a school where every child, regardless of background, can experience the joy of learning and the love of God.

Our Story

From a bold vision to a thriving school community — the Victory journey.

2017

Victory Primary School is Founded

A group of dedicated educators and parents in Ngcobo share a vision: a Christian primary school where every child matters. Victory Primary School opens its doors for the first time, welcoming its founding learners in Grade R through Grade 3.

2018–2019

Growing the Family

Enrolment grows steadily as word spreads through Ngcobo. New classes are added and our teaching staff expands to meet the needs of our growing community. The school establishes its first cultural and sports programmes.

2020–2021

Resilience Through Challenges

Like all schools, Victory Primary faced the challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic with resilience and faith. The school community rallied together to support learners, families, and staff throughout this difficult period.

2022–2023

Full School Status Achieved

Victory Primary welcomes its first full cohort from Grade R through to Grade 7, becoming a complete primary school. This milestone marks the fulfilment of the founders' original vision.

2024–Present

Looking Forward with Faith

Victory Primary continues to grow in strength, reputation, and community impact. We are proud of every learner who has passed through our doors and are excited about the future God has in store for our school.
