Good to know

Common questions

What grades does Victory Primary offer?

We offer Grade R through Grade 7, covering the full primary school journey from the Foundation Phase to the Senior Phase.

Is Victory Primary a fee-paying school?

Yes, we have school fees. We also offer payment arrangements and fee assistance for families who need support. Please speak to the principal in confidence.

Do we need to be Christian to attend?

Our school is founded on Christian values and faith is central to school life, but we welcome all learners whatever their background. We ask families to respect and support our faith-based ethos.

When can I apply for next year?

Applications for the following year usually open mid-year. We recommend applying as early as possible because places are limited. See how to apply.

Where is the school?

Victory Primary School is in Ngcobo, a small town in the Eastern Cape. The map above shows where we are; call us for directions.
