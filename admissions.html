Admissions

We warmly welcome applications for Grade R to Grade 7. Let your child begin their Victory journey today.

2027 Applications Now Open!

We are currently accepting applications for the 2027 academic year. Places are limited — apply early to secure your child's spot at Victory Primary School.

How to Apply

Our admissions process is simple, welcoming, and guided by God's grace. Follow these steps to join the Victory family.

Contact the School

Reach out to our office by phone or email to express your interest and request an application form.

Complete the Form

Fill in the application form fully and accurately. Return it to the school office with all required documents.

Parent Interview

We invite parents and the prospective learner to a brief, friendly meeting with the principal to get to know each other.

Receive Confirmation

Upon acceptance, you'll receive an official letter of placement and your registration pack.

Download Application Form

Download, print, and complete the official Victory Primary School application form. Return the completed form together with all required documents to the school office.

Victory Primary School — Application for Admission

Official 2027 application form. Print, fill in all sections, attach certified documents, and submit to the school office.

Download Application Form (PDF)

Need help? Call us on 072 788 4207 or email victoryprimaryec@gmail.com

Required Documents

Please bring the following documents when submitting your application.

Certified copy of learner's birth certificate
Certified copy of parent/guardian ID
Most recent school report (if applicable)
Transfer card from previous school (if applicable)
Proof of residential address (e.g. utility bill)
Immunisation / Road to Health card
2 recent passport-size photos of the learner
Completed school application form

Age Requirements

The following age guidelines apply when applying for the relevant grade. Ages are as of 1 January of the year of admission.

Grade Minimum Age Phase
Grade R 5 years Foundation Phase
Grade 1 6 years Foundation Phase
Grade 2 7 years Foundation Phase
Grade 3 8 years Foundation Phase
Grade 4 9 years Intermediate Phase
Grade 5 10 years Intermediate Phase
Grade 6 11 years Intermediate Phase
Grade 7 12 years Senior Phase

School Fees

Our fees are kept as affordable as possible in order to serve the Ngcobo community. For detailed fee information, please contact the school office directly.

Fee Assistance Available

We believe that no child should be turned away due to financial constraints. Families in need may apply for a fee reduction or payment arrangement. Speak to the principal in confidence.
