Welcome to Victory

A Place Where Every Child Can Thrive

Situated in the vibrant small town of Ngcobo in the Eastern Cape, Victory Primary School was founded in 2017 on the unshakeable belief that quality education and Christian values can transform lives and communities.

From Grade R through to Grade 7, we walk alongside every learner on their academic journey — building confidence, character, and a lifelong love of learning.

Christ-centred education & values

Our Core Values

Everything we do at Victory Primary is rooted in faith, guided by love, and aimed at building the whole child.

Faith

We centre our school community on God's Word, nurturing spiritual growth in every learner.

Excellence

We encourage each child to pursue their personal best in academics, sport, and the arts.

Community

We are a family. Staff, learners, and parents work together to create a warm, safe environment.

Compassion

We teach children to care for one another and their community through acts of service and kindness.
