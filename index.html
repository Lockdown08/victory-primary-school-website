Principal's welcome

A message from our principal

“At Victory Primary, every child is known by name and loved by God, and we never forget that.”

Welcome to Victory Primary School. Since our founding in 2017, we have been committed to offering the children of Ngcobo an education that is rooted in Christian values and delivered with passion and dedication.

We believe that each learner is uniquely gifted by God, and our role as educators is to help them discover and develop those gifts.

What we stand for

Our four values

Everything at Victory starts with faith and is shaped by love for the whole child.

  1. Faith

    God's Word is at the centre of our school, and we nurture each learner's spiritual growth.

  2. Excellence

    We ask every child for their personal best in the classroom, on the field and on stage.

  3. Community

    Staff, learners and parents work together as one family to keep school warm and safe.

  4. Compassion

    We teach children to care for each other and for Ngcobo through service and kindness.
