Welcome

A small school where every child is known

Victory Primary opened in Ngcobo in 2017 with a simple belief: good teaching and Christian values can change a child's life, and through them, a community.

From their first day in Grade R to their last in Grade 7, we walk alongside every learner, building confidence, character and a love of learning.

  • Christ-centred teaching and values
  • Grade R to 7 in one caring community
  • Sport, culture and the arts alongside the classroom
  • Dedicated, caring teachers
What we stand for

Our four values

Everything at Victory starts with faith and is shaped by love for the whole child.

  1. Faith

    God's Word is at the centre of our school, and we nurture each learner's spiritual growth.

  2. Excellence

    We ask every child for their personal best in the classroom, on the field and on stage.

  3. Community

    Staff, learners and parents work together as one family to keep school warm and safe.

  4. Compassion

    We teach children to care for each other and for Ngcobo through service and kindness.
