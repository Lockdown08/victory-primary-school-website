The calendar for the new year is on its way. Until then, call the office on 069 936 1866 for term dates.

Please note

Term dates follow the Eastern Cape Department of Education calendar and may change. We will let parents know about any updates.

Term 1

14 January – 27 March 2026

  1. 14Jan
    School

    School Opens — Term 1 Begins

    Doors open for the 2026 academic year. All learners report to their classrooms by 07:30.

  2. 21Jan
    Parent

    PTA Meeting

    Parent-Teacher Association meeting. All parents are encouraged to attend and be part of the school community.

  3. 22Jan
    Parent

    Parents Meeting

    A formal meeting for parents to receive school updates, plans, and important information for the term ahead.

  4. 23Jan
    Fundraising

    Casual Dress & Market Day

    Learners may come in casual clothes with a contribution. Join us for a fun-filled fundraising market day.

  5. 2Feb
    Academic

    Formal Assessments Start

    Formal class assessments begin for all grades. Please ensure learners complete all classwork on time.

  6. 2Feb
    Special

    Prefects Campaign Begins

    Grade 7 learners begin campaigning for the position of school prefect — a wonderful opportunity to demonstrate leadership.

  7. 6Feb
    Special

    Election of Prefects

    Learners vote to elect their school prefects for 2026. The school's leadership team for the year is announced.

  8. 13Feb
    Fundraising

    Beauty Contest Fundraising

    A fun school beauty contest fundraising event. Come support our learners and the school!

  9. 26Feb
    Sport

    Athletics Day — Grades 2–7

    Our annual athletics day for Grades 2 to 7. All parents and supporters are warmly welcome to cheer on our athletes.

  10. 27Feb
    Sport

    Athletics Day — Pre R & Grade 1

    Our youngest learners take part in their own special athletics fun day — a memorable event for the little ones!

  11. 6Mar
    Faith

    Easter Celebration

    A special whole-school Easter celebration. We remember and celebrate the resurrection of Jesus Christ together as a school family.

  12. 9Mar
    Academic

    Examinations Start

    End-of-term examinations begin for all grades. Please ensure learners are well-rested and prepared.

  13. 20Mar
    Academic

    Examinations End

    End-of-term examinations conclude for all grades.

  14. 20Mar
    Fundraising

    Human Rights Day Fundraising

    A commemorative fundraising event marking Human Rights Day. Learners are invited to participate and wear the theme colours.

  15. 27Mar
    School

    Reports Issued & School Closes

    Term 1 report cards are issued and school closes for the holidays. School closes at 12:00. Enjoy a well-earned rest!
