Frequently Asked Questions

What grades does Victory Primary offer?

We offer Grade R through Grade 7, covering the full primary school journey from the Foundation Phase right through to the Senior Phase.

Is Victory Primary a fee-paying school?

Yes, we have a school fee structure in place. However, we offer payment arrangements and fee assistance for families who need support. Please speak to the principal confidentially.

Do I need to be Christian to attend Victory Primary?

While our school is founded on Christian values and faith is central to our school life, we welcome all learners regardless of background. We ask that families respect and support our faith-based ethos.

When can I apply for the next school year?

Applications for the following year typically open mid-year. We recommend applying as early as possible as spaces are limited. Contact us for current availability.

Where is the school located?

Victory Primary School is located in Ngcobo, a small town in the Eastern Cape province of South Africa. Contact us for the exact street address and directions.
