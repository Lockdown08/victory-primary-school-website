Academics

A solid academic foundation built on faith, curiosity, and the joy of discovery — from Grade R through Grade 7.

Our Curriculum

Victory Primary School follows the South African CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement) curriculum, enriched with Christian values and integrated learning.

CAPS Aligned

We fully follow the national CAPS curriculum, ensuring our learners are well-prepared for high school and beyond.

Faith-Integrated

Christian values and biblical principles are woven naturally into daily lessons, devotions, and school life.

Learner-Centred

We teach to every learner's unique strengths, giving each child the attention and support they need to succeed.

Multilingual

We celebrate and support our learners' home languages while developing strong English and isiXhosa literacy.

Extra-Curricular Activities

At Victory Primary, we believe in developing the whole child — in and out of the classroom.

Sport

Football, netball, athletics, and more — sport teaches teamwork, discipline, and healthy competition.

Creative Arts

Drama, visual arts, and crafts give our learners a creative outlet and build confidence.

Choir & Music

Our school choir praises God and represents Victory Primary at community and district events.

Reading Club

We nurture a love of reading through our weekly reading club and well-stocked school library.
